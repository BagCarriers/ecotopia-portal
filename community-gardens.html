Growing together

Community gardens & food forests

Some of the most rewarding work we do happens on shared ground. Alongside neighbors and volunteers, we build and tend community gardens and food forests, like the Reciprocity Community Food Forest on Browning Avenue, where native plantings feed pollinators and people alike.

These spaces double as living classrooms for native plants, permaculture, and habitat, and they are open to anyone willing to lend a hand.
