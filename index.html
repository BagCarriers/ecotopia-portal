What we do

Landscapes that give back

Every design starts with the native plants and living systems that belong here, then layers in beauty, food, and habitat for the pollinators and people who share your land.

🦋

Pollinator Garden & Mini Meadow

Native perennial wildflowers that bloom across three seasons and draw butterflies, hummingbirds, bees, and beneficial insects.

🌳

Food Forest Design

Edible landscaping designed with permaculture techniques, planted in vertical layers for an abundance of perennial food.

🧺

Living Willow Fence

Willow branches planted and woven in March into living fences, tunnels, domes, and archways.

💧

Rain Garden

A planted depression filled with well-drained soil and deep-rooted perennial flowers that soak up seasonal water.

🌿

Medicinal Herb Garden

Medicinal plants chosen for your land and health needs, with consulting and implementation.

🌾

Lawn to Meadow Conversion

You may qualify for a free conversion through the PA DCNR program we contract for (half acre minimum).
