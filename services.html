Services

Ecological landscaping, start to season

We design and plant living systems suited to your land and the wildlife of Central Pennsylvania. Explore the eleven ways we can help your ground come alive.

01

Pollinator Garden / Mini Meadow

Planted with a diverse array of native perennial wildflowers that bloom across three seasons. Attracts butterflies, hummingbirds, bees, and beneficial insects.

02

Food Forest Design

Edible landscaping designed with permaculture techniques, planted in vertical layers for an abundance of perennial food.

03

Rain Garden

A planted depression filled with well-drained soil and deep-rooted perennial flowers that soak up seasonal water.

04

Annual Food Garden

Garden planning and design, soil analysis and improvement, plant selection, pest and disease management, sustainable practices.

05

Living Willow Fence

Willow branches planted and woven in March into living fences, tunnels, domes, and archways.

06

Routine Garden Maintenance

Knowledgeable ecological landscapers who know which plant is an invasive weed and which is a native you planted.

07

Medicinal Herb Garden and Consulting

Medicinal plants chosen for your land and health needs, with consulting and implementation.

08

Forest Habitat Restoration

Riparian buffer planting and invasive plant removal for biodiverse habitat.

09

Woodland Habitat Restoration

Thinning canopy trees to bring sunlight to the forest floor, adding understory trees, shrubs, vines, herbs, and woodland flowers.

10

Tree Nets

Hand woven rope nets suspended between living trees: part hammock, part treehouse floor, part aerial hangout. Woven on site to fit your trees.

11 · Lawn to Meadow Conversion

You may qualify for a free meadow

We contract for a PA DCNR program that can convert your lawn to native meadow at no cost to you. A half acre minimum applies. Ask us whether your land qualifies.
