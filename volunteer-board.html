Open Responsibilities

These tasks need a regular volunteer. Each one has an estimated time and cadence. If something feels right for your schedule, tap "I'll take this" to sign up.

Not ready to commit to a recurring task?

No problem. Every community garden keeps a list of walk-in tasks: simple, do-it-when-you-can jobs like pulling weeds, picking up trash, or watering if it is dry. Help with one whenever you happen to be there.

  1. Stop by any of our community gardens whenever suits you.
  2. Scan the QR code on the garden sign, or open the garden's task list below.
  3. Pick a walk-in task, do it, and check in so we know the garden got some love.
